About OPEN-Quantum

What We Built & Why

OPEN-Quantum is an interactive, browser-based semiconductor physics simulator that transforms traditionally static textbook concepts — Fermi-Dirac statistics, Kronig-Penney band theory, Brillouin zones, Drude transport, p-n junctions, MOSFET characteristics, phonon dispersion, and reciprocal lattice geometry — into live, parameter-driven visualizations backed by real physics equations. We built it because semiconductor physics sits at the exact intersection of quantum mechanics and modern computing, yet most learners encounter it only through dense equations and flat diagrams; by making every parameter tunable via sliders and every plot instantly responsive, we collapse the gap between formula and physical intuition. The animated circuit-board interface, twenty-five distinct plots, 1st-through-10th Brillouin zone mapper, and an AI chatbot (PhysBot) that retrieves live knowledge from Wikipedia and ArXiv on every query together create a single cohesive environment where a student, researcher, or engineer can explore, question, and understand the physics that underlies every transistor on earth.
